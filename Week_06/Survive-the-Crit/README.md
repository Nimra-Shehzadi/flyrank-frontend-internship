# Survive the Crit — Week 6

## Overview

This task focused on reviewing my live personal portfolio from a visitor's perspective and using external feedback to identify areas that could be improved.

The goal was not to redesign the portfolio unnecessarily, but to understand whether the website clearly communicates who I am, what I do, and whether my projects provide enough evidence of my development skills.

## Portfolio Reviewed

**Live Portfolio:**
https://flyrank-frontend-internship-hfd2.vercel.app/

## Review Questions

The review was based on two key questions:

1. In ten seconds, what do I do?
2. Would you believe I am good at it? Why or why not?

Additional feedback was collected regarding clarity, project presentation, navigation, and the overall first impression.

## Feedback Summary

The portfolio successfully communicates that I am a Computer Science student and developer with practical experience in frontend and full-stack development.

The projects and technology sections provide evidence of hands-on development work. However, the review identified opportunities to make the portfolio's main message, project information, and primary actions clearer.

## Must-Fix Items

* Make the introduction more direct about my role and development focus.
* Make the primary portfolio action easier to identify.
* Improve project descriptions so their purpose, technologies, and contribution are immediately clear.
* Make important information easier to scan.

## Nice-to-Have Items

* Add detailed project case studies in the future.
* Add more live project demonstrations.
* Introduce subtle animations and micro-interactions.
* Continue improving the visual hierarchy as the portfolio grows.

## Changes Made

The must-fix feedback was reviewed and prioritized based on its impact on the portfolio's clarity and credibility.

The improvements focused on communicating my developer identity more clearly, presenting project information more effectively, and making the visitor's next action easier to understand.

## Key Learning

This review showed me that a portfolio should not only look professional; it should also communicate its purpose quickly.

Getting feedback from another perspective helps identify problems that can easily become invisible when working on the same website for a long time.

## Deliverables

* Portfolio review feedback
* Must-fix vs. nice-to-have classification
* Updated portfolio based on priority feedback
* Live portfolio evidence
